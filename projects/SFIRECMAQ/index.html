Surface PM2.5 concentration. The four panels compare simulated PM2.5 across Freitas-SFIRE-CMAQ, WRF-SFIRE-Chem, SFIRE-FOAM-CMAQ, and SFIRE-CMAQ. Colors show surface PM2.5 (µg m−3), and arrows indicate surface wind direction and speed.
